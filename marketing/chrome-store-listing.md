## 2. Chrome Web Store Detailed Description (Markdown)

```markdown
# 🛡️ Protect Your Etsy Shop from Costly Trademark Strikes & DMCA Takedowns

Did you know that common everyday phrases like **"Boy Mom"**, **"Mama Bear"**, **"Onesie"**, **"Velcro"**, and **"Super Bowl"** are actively trademarked and heavily enforced by predatory IP owners?

A single accidental trademark infringement can result in **listing deactivation, payment account reserves, or permanent Etsy shop suspension**.

**ListSafe Guard** is your real-time IP defense assistant that runs directly inside your Etsy & Shopify listing editors, protecting your livelihood 24/7.

---

### ⚡ Key Features

✨ **Real-Time Trademark Detection**
As you type your Listing Title, Tags, and Description, ListSafe instantly flags high-risk words, displaying the registered trademark owner, registration class (e.g. Class 025 Apparel), and risk level.

✨ **1-Click Smart Safe Replacements**
Never guess what words are safe. Click any suggested alternative (e.g., replace *"Boy Mom"* with *"Mother of Boys"*) to update your listing text instantly.

✨ **Etsy Tags Compliance & Character Counter**
Automatically verifies that your tags adhere to Etsy's 13-tag limit and 20-character maximum per tag.

✨ **Listing Health Safety Score (0–100)**
Get an immediate visual safety rating before you click "Publish". Aim for 100/100 to guarantee risk-free publishing!

✨ **Instant IP Lookup Database**
Search through hundreds of high-risk terms in our curated 2026 database categorized by Class 025 (Apparel), Class 014 (Jewelry), Class 028 (Toys/Games), and more.

✨ **Custom Brand Whitelist**
Add your own registered trademarks or authorized brand names so they are never flagged as errors.

---

### 🔍 Works Seamlessly On:
- Etsy Listing Manager (New & Classic Editor)
- Shopify Product Editor
- Amazon Merch on Demand & KDP
- Standalone IP Search Popup

---

### 💡 Why Solo Sellers Love ListSafe:
- **Zero Tab Switching**: No need to waste hours navigating clunky USPTO / TESS databases.
- **Privacy First**: ListSafe runs locally on your browser. Your listing drafts, pricing, and secret keywords are NEVER sent to third-party ad networks.
- **Lightweight & Blazing Fast**: Built on Google Chrome's latest Manifest V3 standard with zero browser slowdown.

---

### 🔒 Disclaimer
ListSafe is an independent compliance assistant created to help sellers conduct trademark due diligence. ListSafe is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Etsy, Inc., Shopify, or the USPTO.
```
