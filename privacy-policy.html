ListSafe Guard Compliance

Privacy Policy for ListSafe Extension

Last Updated: August 17, 2026 • Applicable Version: Manifest V3 (v1.0.x) • Status: Active & Compliant

1. Overview & Single Purpose

ListSafe - Etsy IP & Trademark Infringement Guard ("ListSafe", "we", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains our data practices in full compliance with the Google Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies, GDPR, and CCPA.

🛡️ Zero Data Transmission Guarantee: ListSafe performs 100% of its trademark scanning and risk analysis locally on your browser. Your listing drafts, keywords, and text are NEVER sent to external servers or third-party ad networks.

2. Data We DO NOT Collect

We adhere to strict "Privacy by Design" principles. ListSafe does NOT collect, store, sell, or transmit:

  • No Personally Identifiable Information (PII): No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or IP addresses are gathered.
  • No Listing Content or Trade Secrets: Your listing titles, descriptions, tag strategies, and pricing remain exclusively in your local browser memory.
  • No Keystroke Logging: We do not log, capture, or transmit keystrokes. Input is evaluated strictly against our local regex database in real time.
  • No Browsing History or Search Logs: We do not monitor, record, or track your browsing activity across the web.
  • No Payment Details: Pro license purchases are processed via external, PCI-compliant payment providers (such as Stripe / Lemon Squeezy). We never receive your credit card credentials.

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

ListSafe uses Chrome's local storage APIs (chrome.storage.local) exclusively to save your personal preferences:

  • User Interface Preferences: Your selected interface language (English, Chinese, German, etc.) and scan trigger modes.
  • Custom Whitelist: Brand terms or safe keywords you have explicitly authorized.
  • Pro License Cache: An encrypted local token verifying your Pro status to avoid recurring verification calls.
  • Local Usage Counters: Aggregated numbers of scanned items and blocked risks, stored strictly on your device.

4. Chrome Extension Permissions Justification

Permission Justification
storage Stores local settings, custom whitelisted words, and Pro license status on your device.
activeTab / scripting Allows ListSafe to inspect the active listing editor DOM and highlight trademark risks upon your interaction.
host_permissions Strictly limited to supported e-commerce domains (e.g., etsy.com, shopify.com) to execute content scripts only where needed.

5. Third-Party Sharing & Disclosure

We do NOT sell, trade, rent, or transfer any user data to third parties, advertising networks, data brokers, or analytics platforms under any circumstances.

6. Data Deletion & User Control

Because all configuration data is stored locally within your browser, you retain complete control over your data. You can delete all data at any time by right-clicking the ListSafe extension icon and selecting "Remove from Chrome" or clearing extension data via Chrome Settings.

1. 概述与单一用途

ListSafe - Etsy 跨境电商商标风险拦截插件（下称“ListSafe”或“我们”）高度重视您的隐私保护。本政策严格遵循 Google Chrome 网上应用店开发者计划政策、GDPR 及通用数据安全规范。

🛡️ 零数据外传承诺： ListSafe 的所有商标词库匹配与风险评估逻辑均 100% 在您的本地浏览器中完成。您的 Listing 草稿、标题、标签与商业关键词绝不会上传至任何远程服务器或第三方广告平台。

2. 我们绝不收集的数据

我们坚持“从设计阶段保障隐私 (Privacy by Design)”原则，ListSafe 绝不收集、存储、出售或传输以下任何信息：

  • 绝无个人身份信息 (PII)： 不收集姓名、邮箱、电话号码或网络 IP 地址。
  • 绝无商品 Listing 与商业秘密： 您的商品标题、描述、标签组合及定价完全保留在您的本地浏览器内存中，绝不上报。
  • 绝无按键记录： 插件仅在本地通过正则与规则库即时匹配违规词，绝不记录或导出您的输入行为。
  • 绝无浏览历史记录： 我们不跟踪、不记录您的网络访问日志及其他网站行为。
  • 绝无银行与支付信息： Pro 会员订阅完全通过具备 PCI 认证的合规支付通道（如 Stripe / Lemon Squeezy / Waffo）完成，我们无法获知您的银行卡信息。

3. 本地存储的数据

ListSafe 仅利用 Chrome 本地存储 API (chrome.storage.local) 在您的设备本地保存基本配置：

  • 用户界面偏好： 语言选择（中文、英文等）及自动扫描开关。
  • 自定义白名单： 您主动添加的已授权自有品牌词。
  • Pro 激活凭证： 加密存储的激活状态令牌，避免重复联网核验。
  • 本地体检统计： 仅在本地记录的已扫描词数与已拦截风险数。

4. 权限使用说明

权限名称 使用用途及合理性

在任何情况下，我们都绝不向第三方、数据经纪商、广告商或分析平台出售、出租或共享任何用户数据。

由于所有数据均储存于您的本地浏览器，您拥有完整的控制权。在 Chrome 扩展管理界面右键选择“从 Chrome 中移除”即可一键彻底清除所有本地数据。
